About

Motsoalle means “friend”

A friend who knows the courses, the bursaries and the deadlines, and who answers in your own language, any time.

We’re economists and technologists who went through the move from high school to higher learning ourselves, with little guidance. We built Motsoalle so the next generation of Basotho school leavers doesn’t have to guess.

How we got here

  1. Listening to students

    We interviewed students. All 11 said the problem matters and that today’s options don’t work for them. They asked for one trustworthy, up-to-date place for the move to higher learning.

  2. A closed pilot at The Hub Morija

    From October 2024, more than 120 students used Motsoalle in our first pilot. They asked most about careers, scholarships and which course or institution to choose.

  3. Open to every student

    We opened Motsoalle through social media and grew to more than 780 users. 65% came back to continue the conversation after getting their action steps.

  4. Recognised by the African Union

    Motsoalle was selected among the Top 10 Innovators in the African Union’s Innovating Education in Africa (IEA) 2025 Programme.

  5. Next: every district

    By April 2027 we aim to reach schools in all 10 districts, 3,000 active students and our first paying partners.
