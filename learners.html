For learners

Know your next step after school

Whether you’re in your final year or have just finished, Motsoalle helps you find a course that fits your results, the bursaries you can apply for, and the deadlines you can’t miss.

Start in three steps

  1. Save the number

    Add +266 5809 8105 to your contacts as Motsoalle, or tap the button below.

  2. Say hi

    Send “Hi” or “Lumela”. Motsoalle replies in Sesotho or English.

  3. Choose what you need

    Tap Career Guidance or Institution Finder, or type your own question.

Questions you can ask

Ask the way you would ask a friend. Here are a few to get you started.

  • Which courses can I study with my results?

    Tell Motsoalle your LGCSE results and what you enjoy.

  • What do I need to apply for this programme?

    Entry requirements, fees and closing dates, with the source.

  • Which bursaries can I apply for?

    Bursaries that match your plans, and when to apply.

  • Which jobs will be in demand?

    Careers with growing demand, so your studies lead somewhere.

  • How should I prepare for my exams?

    Study tips drawn from ECoL examiners’ reports.

Questions learners ask us

Does it cost anything?

Motsoalle is free for students. You only use your normal WhatsApp data, and Motsoalle is mostly text so it uses very little.

Which languages can I use?

Sesotho and English. Write in whichever you’re comfortable with.

Who is Motsoalle for?

Mainly final-year students and school leavers aged about 16 to 22 in Lesotho, especially in rural areas where career guidance is hard to find. Parents and teachers are welcome to use it too.

Can I trust the information?

Motsoalle only uses official, verified sources, and a person approves every update. Each answer shows where the information came from and when it was checked. If Motsoalle doesn’t have a verified answer, it tells you and gives you the institution’s contact. Before paying any fee, always confirm with the institution.

Will I see adverts?

Some messages may come from sponsors who help keep Motsoalle free. They are always labelled as sponsored, and sponsors never change the advice Motsoalle gives you.

What happens to my messages?

We never sell your personal data. Any insights we share with education bodies are combined and anonymised, so no one can tell who said what. Read our privacy summary.

Does Motsoalle replace my teacher or a career counsellor?

No. It gives you clear, checked information any time, so your conversations with teachers, family and counsellors start from facts.
