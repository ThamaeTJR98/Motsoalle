Choosing what to study shouldn’t come down to luck.

Most final-year students in rural Lesotho make the biggest decision of their lives with little counselling and information scattered across prospectuses, websites and WhatsApp groups. Choices made blind feed a skills mismatch, and the cost shows up years later as wasted fees and unemployment.

21,117

students sat the LGCSE in 2025. Every one of them faces this choice.

Lesotho News Agency, 2025
3 in 4

Lesotho graduates face unemployment linked to a skills mismatch.

CHE Lesotho, National Skills Audit 2021/22
~10%

gross enrolment in tertiary education.

UNESCO Institute for Statistics, 2018

What you can ask Motsoalle

Tap a button or type your question. Motsoalle answers with the course, the entry rules, the cost, the bursary and the deadline, and tells you where the information came from.

How to get started

  • Career Guidance

    Paths that fit your LGCSE results and where jobs are growing.

  • Institution Finder

    Programmes, entry requirements, fees and deadlines.

  • Scholarship Wizard

    Bursaries you may qualify for, and when to apply.

  • Study Abroad Guide

    Options beyond Lesotho, explained simply.

  • Exam prep tips

    Study tips drawn from ECoL examiners’ reports.

From scattered information to a plan you can act on

  1. Official sources

    Prospectuses, institution pages and official group notices.

  2. Checked by a person

    Every update is approved before students see it.

  3. Dated and sourced

    Each fact keeps its source and the date it was checked.

  4. Matched to you

    Built around your own results and interests.

  5. Your next steps

    Course, entry rules, cost, bursary and deadline.

When Motsoalle has no verified source, it says so and gives you the institution’s contact instead of guessing. See exactly where our information comes from.
