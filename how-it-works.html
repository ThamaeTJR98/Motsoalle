How it works

Where Motsoalle’s information comes from

Good guidance depends on good information. Here is exactly what Motsoalle knows, where it looks, who checks it and what it does when it isn’t sure.

From a question to a checked plan

  1. We gather official sources

    Prospectuses, institution websites, bursary calls and notices from official WhatsApp groups.

  2. A person checks every update

    Nothing reaches students until a member of our team approves it.

  3. Each fact keeps its source and date

    So students can see where an answer came from and how recent it is.

  4. Motsoalle matches it to the student

    Using the student’s LGCSE results, interests and the job market.

  5. The student gets clear next steps

    The course, entry rules, cost, bursary and deadline, in Sesotho or English.

What Motsoalle knows, and where it looks

Knowledge base

Curated and checked, every item dated

  • Official prospectuses of Lesotho institutions: programmes, entry requirements, fees and deadlines
  • Lesotho’s higher education prospectus
  • Official bursary and scholarship calls
  • World Economic Forum (WEF) Future of Jobs Report 2025
  • Council on Higher Education (CHE) National Skills Audit Baseline Study 2021/22
  • Examinations Council of Lesotho (ECoL) examiners’ reports 2019–2024, for exam prep

Live sources

Looked up again, so advice stays current

  • Verified official websites of institutions
  • Verified official social media pages of institutions and bursary bodies
  • Admin messages in official WhatsApp groups
  • Peer WhatsApp groups, only where the admin agrees, used for trends only, with no names

Never used: general web scraping, unverified pages or private messages.

Pointing students to tomorrow’s jobs

A course only pays off if it leads to work. Motsoalle weighs what students want against where jobs are growing, using the WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025 and Lesotho’s national skills audit.

Because it looks information up again instead of relying on a frozen database, advice keeps up with new programmes, fees and deadlines.

People behind every update

Knowledge-base updates are approved by Reitumetse Sehloho, Motsoalle’s co-founder, and Meri Hyoky, coordinator at The Hub Morija.

No verified source, no guess. Motsoalle tells the student it doesn’t know and gives the institution’s contact.

What could go wrong, and how we handle it

RiskHow we handle it
Wrong or outdated adviceOfficial sources only, every update approved by a person, source and date shown, and honest “I don’t know” answers.
Peer WhatsApp groupsUsed only where the admin agrees, for trends only, with no names or private messages.
Data protectionCombined, anonymised data only, in line with the Lesotho Data Protection Act 2011.
Sponsors buying influenceSponsors never change the advice, and sponsored messages are always labelled.
