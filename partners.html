Why partner with us

65% of students came back to continue the conversation after getting their action steps. Motsoalle is a place they return to while they decide.

Reached at decision time

Students arrive when they’re choosing a course, applying and working out how to pay. That’s when a youth account, a data bundle or a bursary matters most.

On the app they already use

Trust built in

Advice comes from checked official sources. Sponsored content is always labelled and never changes the advice, which is why students trust what they see.

What an insight report can show

Education bodies are responsible for a smooth move from school to higher learning, but today they can’t see what students struggle with before they apply. Reports could show:

Course demand vs jobs

What students want to study, against where jobs are growing.

Entry barriers

Requirements students most often fall short of.

Funding gaps

Bursary questions and missed deadlines.

Drop-off points

Where students stop in the application journey.

District gaps

How needs differ between rural and town schools.

Timing

When in the year questions and worry peak.

We will agree the exact contents of reports with each partner during a first pilot.

How onboarding works

From first contact to going live, here is what happens.

Information and school partners usually skip steps 3 and 6, since those partnerships are free.

  1. Tell us about your organisation

    Send the enquiry form below, or email us. Tell us what you’d like to achieve.

  2. Introductory call

    We reply by email to set up a short call, explain how Motsoalle works and understand your goals.

  3. Agree a pilot

    We agree the scope together: a placement package or an insight report, the timeline, what we’ll report, and the price.

  4. Prepare and check content

    You share your message or questions. We check them against our partner rules and label anything sponsored.

  5. Go live

    Your placement appears in the chat, or we begin collecting the anonymised patterns for your report.

  6. Review and renew

    You receive your monthly reach report or insight report. We review results together and decide what comes next.

How much does it cost?

Sponsored placements start from USD 250 a month during our pilot. Insight report pricing is agreed in a first pilot. School and information partnerships are free.

Will we receive student data?

No. Partners never receive names, numbers or conversations. Insight reports use combined, anonymised data only.

Can we choose where our placement appears?

Placements appear beside questions related to what you offer, for example a youth account beside questions about paying fees. We agree topics during the pilot.

How do we know it worked?

Sponsors receive a monthly report on how many students their placement reached. We review results together before renewing.
