Privacy

How we handle your information

A plain-language summary. Last updated November 2026.

What we receive

When you chat with Motsoalle on WhatsApp, we receive your WhatsApp number and the messages you send, so that Motsoalle can answer you. On this website, we only receive what you choose to send through the partner enquiry form.

How we use it

We use your messages to give you guidance and to improve the quality of that guidance. We use partner enquiry details only to reply to you about a partnership.

What we never do

We never sell your personal data. Partners and sponsors never receive your name, your number or your conversations.

Anonymised insights

We may share combined, anonymised patterns with education bodies, for example which courses many students ask about, or when bursary questions peak. These reports never identify an individual student.

Sponsored messages

Some messages in the chat may come from sponsors who help keep Motsoalle free. They are always labelled as sponsored, and they never change the guidance Motsoalle gives you.

The law we follow

We handle personal data in line with the Lesotho Data Protection Act 2011.

Questions or requests

To ask about your information, email motsoalle@trwebsolutions.com. Motsoalle is run by T&R Analytics and Web Designs (Pty) Ltd, Maseru, Lesotho.
